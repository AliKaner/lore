Word lists for completion (tr.txt, en.txt)

Derived from FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
(content/2018/tr/tr_50k.txt and content/2018/en/en_50k.txt,
built from OpenSubtitles 2018, http://www.opensubtitles.org)

Changes: kept words of four or more letters, in order of frequency, at
most 40,000 per language; dropped entries with digits or punctuation,
foreign letters, slurs and vulgarities; lowercased; frequencies removed.
