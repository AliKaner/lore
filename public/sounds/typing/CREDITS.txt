Booktion typing sounds
======================

Each file in this folder is cut from the recordings below: silence trimmed,
levels evened out, mixed to mono 32 kHz and joined into one file per sound.

typewriter.wav
  Key strikes: "Typewriter sounds" by Cassie-OrbitGames, CC0
  https://opengameart.org/content/typewriter-sounds
  Line-end bell (pitched up): "Bell dings/chimes", CC0
  https://opengameart.org/content/bell-dingschimes

soft.wav
  "Keyboard Soundpack #1 [Typing and Single Keystrokes]" by unicaegames, CC0
  (Cherry KC 1000)
  https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes

pencil.wav
  "Pencil Sounds", CC0 (pencil_write by NachtmahrTV, pencil_erase by damsur)
  https://opengameart.org/content/pencil-sounds

mechanical-brown.wav, mechanical-blue.wav
  Cherry MX Brown PBT and Cherry MX Blue PBT sound packs from Mechvibes,
  MIT License: https://github.com/hainguyents13/mechvibes
